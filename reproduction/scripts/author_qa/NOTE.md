# Author-side quality assurance

These two scripts check the manuscript and the response letters against the
artifacts. They need the LaTeX source and the letter drafts, which are not in
this bundle, so they will not run here and `reproduce.py` does not call them.

They are included because they are part of what was released with the paper,
and because the checks they encode are the ones that caught several of the
errors the revision corrects.
